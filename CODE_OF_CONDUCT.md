# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for contributors and users.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about technical issues.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions or interactions that violate these expectations.
